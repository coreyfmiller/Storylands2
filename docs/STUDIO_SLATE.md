# The studio slate

Premises that have no book yet.

These twelve were catalog entries once: full covers, hooks and synopses sitting on
the shelf, each one opening an apology when a child tapped it. Twelve of nineteen
titles were a facade, and two of the four reader profiles could open nothing at
all. They are off the shelf now.

The writing is good, so it is kept here as input rather than deleted. A premise on
this page is one command away from being a real book:

```
node scripts/create-book.mjs --character <id> --premise "<the premise below>"
```

Two things to settle before using one.

Each needs a **protagonist with approved reference art** — `node scripts/doctor.mjs`
lists the cast. Several of these were written around human children, who have none,
and a character cannot be drawn on-model without a reference.

And most were written for ages 8 and up, while every rule in the pipeline targets
4 to 7: the spine, the word budget, the happy forward-looking ending. An older
premise needs recasting for the younger reader, not lifting as-is. "Seven Minutes
Missing" is a lovely idea and it is not a picture book.

## Ages 8-10

### The Last Firefly

**Summer ends tomorrow. Tonight there is still time for one small adventure.**

On the final night of summer, Emi finds a single firefly glowing brighter than all the rest — and clearly lost. Helping it home means crossing the whole field in the dark, and the field is far bigger after sunset than it ever is by day.

*Was listed as Ages 9+, 15 min, Adventure / Heart.*

### Juniper's Moonlight Delivery

**One parcel. One moonlit forest. Several shortcuts that probably aren't shortcuts.**

Juniper has one job tonight: deliver a small glowing parcel to the far side of the woods before the moon sets. The main path is long. The shortcuts are tempting. And the forest, it turns out, has opinions about which way she should go.

*Was listed as Ages 8+, 18 min, Adventure / Friendship.*

### The Thing Under the Shed

**Something keeps stealing Nora's strawberries. Tonight she's waiting.**

Every morning another strawberry is gone. Nora has a flashlight, a blanket, and a plan to stay awake all night until she catches the thief. What she finds under the shed is stranger — and gentler — than she expected.

*Was listed as Ages 8+, 15 min, Mystery / Discovery.*

### Maple's Very Bad Camping Idea

**The backyard is twenty steps from home. At night, it feels considerably farther.**

Maple was sure about the plan at noon. A tent, a torch, a whole night of adventure in the backyard. But the dark has a way of stretching twenty steps into twenty miles, and every ordinary sound now needs investigating.

*Was listed as Ages 8+, 15 min, Adventure / Comedy.*

## Ages 11-13

### The Lighthouse at Grey Point

**Three friends discover a light flashing from a lighthouse abandoned for thirty years.**

Grey Point lighthouse has been dark since before any of them were born. So when the beam sweeps the cliffs one stormy evening, Sam, Priya and Theo have a choice: pretend they never saw it, or find out who — or what — turned it back on.

*Was listed as Ages 11+, 30 min, Mystery / Adventure.*

### The Map That Wasn't There Yesterday

**A hand-drawn map appears inside Leo's locker. It shows a place underneath the school.**

No one admits to leaving it. The paper is old, the ink is fresh, and the map is unmistakably of the school — plus three floors that shouldn't exist. Leo has until the last bell to decide whether some doors are better left unopened.

*Was listed as Ages 11+, 25 min, Mystery / Adventure.*

### Seven Minutes Missing

**Every clock in town skipped exactly seven minutes. Maya seems to be the only one who noticed.**

At 8:14 the whole town blinked forward to 8:21. No one remembers the gap. No one but Maya, who counted every second of it. Now she has to work out where seven minutes go when an entire town agrees to forget them.

*Was listed as Ages 11+, 30 min, Mystery / Sci-Fi.*

### The Last Train to Bellweather

**The train shouldn't stop here. Tonight it did.**

Bellweather station closed decades ago, but the 11:50 has just pulled in, doors open, lamps warm against the fog. It is waiting. The only question left is whether to step aboard and find out where a train goes when it stops somewhere it shouldn't.

*Was listed as Ages 12+, 35 min, Adventure / Mystery.*

## Ages 14-16

### Vanished

**The police think they left. You don't.**

Yesterday they were here. Today their phone is off and their side of the room is cold. Everyone has decided this is simple. You are the only one still asking the questions that don't have simple answers.

*Was listed as Ages 14+, 45 min, Mystery / Thriller.*

### Black River

**Something happened near Black River. Everyone remembers it differently.**

The town agrees on the date and nothing else. Six people, six versions, one stretch of dark water that keeps its own counsel. The truth is somewhere between the stories — if you can stand to line them up beside each other.

*Was listed as Ages 14+, 45 min, Mystery / Suspense.*

### Signal Lost

**Only four appear in the photograph taken that night.**

Five friends hiked past the last cell tower for the quiet. They got it. The photo from the ridge shows the sunset, the treeline, and four of them — and no one can explain the empty space where the fifth was standing.

*Was listed as Ages 14+, 40 min, Mystery / Adventure.*

### Room 217

**The hotel says the room doesn't exist. Your key says otherwise.**

The front desk is polite and completely certain: there is no 217. The corridor disagrees. So does the brass key warming in your hand. Between the second and third floor there is a door, and behind it a night that never quite ends.

*Was listed as Ages 13+, 35 min, Mystery / Suspense.*
